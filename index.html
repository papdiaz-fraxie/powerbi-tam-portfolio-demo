How I work

Operational substance first, tools second

The common thread is not a particular industry. It is making imperfect data and workflows usable, testable and decision-ready.

01 · UnderstandStart with the business decision

Clarify what needs to change, who uses the output and what would make the result trustworthy.

02 · ControlMake data quality explicit

Standardise inputs, define matching or attribution rules, surface exceptions and preserve evidence.

03 · AutomateRemove repeat manual handling

Use SQL, Python, Apps Script, APIs and workflow tools where automation improves repeatability.

04 · DecideTranslate analysis into action

Normalise metrics, challenge misleading results and communicate what the evidence supports.
