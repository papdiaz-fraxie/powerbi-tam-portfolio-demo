Market Intelligence & TAM Analysis

A client-facing service that translated targeting criteria into a measurable addressable market, then into geographic and commercial segments that sales and marketing teams could act on.

Client problem

How large is the real target market?

An Australian telecommunications client wanted to understand the size and structure of its potential market across Australia before committing campaign resources.

The supplied report identified 37,993 potentially addressable companies and broke the market down by state, industry and employee-size band.

Business value

From a broad brief to a prioritised market.

The value was not simply providing a count. The analysis turned broad targeting requirements into a measurable market and then into segments that could be prioritised for sales and campaign activity.

What I did

Translated targeting criteria into usable analysis.

  • Converted client ICP and targeting requirements into practical data filters.
  • Analysed large B2B datasets, including around 1M+ records across broader client work.
  • Broke addressable markets down by geography, industry and employee size.
  • Investigated buying triggers and technology-install / technographic information where relevant.
  • Analysed campaign and engagement performance after activation to understand which segments produced stronger outcomes.
  • Presented findings in client-facing reporting so commercial teams could refine where they focused effort.
Questions answered

What commercial teams could decide

  • Which states contain most of the opportunity?
  • Which industries should be prioritised first?
  • Which employee-size bands are large enough to support a campaign?
  • How much does the market shrink as additional targeting criteria are applied?
  • Which technology or market signals are useful for account selection?
  • Which campaign segments generate engagement versus qualified outcomes?
