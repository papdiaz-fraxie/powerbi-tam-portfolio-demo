Why the caveat matters: 4–5 pm looks strong in the recorded data, but delayed end-of-day CRM administration may inflate that hour. The evidence is not strong enough to call it the definitive best calling time.
Executive conclusion

Channel choice mattered more clearly than timing.

Mobile / Direct / Personal calls consistently produced stronger qualified rates than Switch / Landline calls. Reduced fresh mobile supply led to more repeat calling without an observed deterioration in conversion. The apparent late-afternoon timing advantage remains provisional because CRM update behaviour can distort the attribution.
